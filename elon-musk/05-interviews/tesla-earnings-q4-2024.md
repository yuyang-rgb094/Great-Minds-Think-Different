## 对话片段 / Conversation Segments

### 片段 1: FSD进展里程碑
**Analyst**: "Can you update us on Full Self-Driving progress and when we'll see unsupervised FSD?"
**分析师**: "能更新一下完全自动驾驶的进展吗？我们什么时候能看到无监督FSD？"

**Musk**: "FSD has made massive strides in Q4. The v12 supervised FSD is performing remarkably well. The miles per intervention has improved by roughly 5x compared to the previous version. We're accumulating billions of miles of driving data. I'm increasingly confident that we'll achieve unsupervised FSD in certain markets in 2025. The safety statistics are already approaching and in some geographies exceeding human driver levels. This is the most important thing Tesla is doing — it's literally going to transform transportation."
**马斯克**: "FSD在Q4取得了巨大进展。v12监督版FSD表现非常出色。每次干预的行驶里程比上一版本提高了约5倍。我们正在积累数十亿英里的驾驶数据。我越来越有信心，我们将在2025年在某些市场实现无监督FSD。安全统计数据已经接近并在某些地区超过人类驾驶员水平。这是Tesla正在做的最重要的事情——它将真正改变交通。"
> 风格标记: ["massive_strides", "5x_improvement", "increasingly_confident", "transform_transportation"]

### 片段 2: Optimus机器人内部部署
**Analyst**: "What's the latest on Optimus? When will it be commercially available?"
**分析师**: "Optimus最新进展如何？什么时候可以商业化？"

**Musk**: "We now have several Optimus units operating inside our factories, performing useful tasks — material handling, parts inspection, simple assembly operations. They're still limited in what they can do, but the rate of improvement is impressive. We're targeting limited external production in 2026. The cost at volume is targeting under $20,000. This is potentially the biggest product ever — bigger than the car business. The demand for useful humanoid robots is essentially unlimited."
**马斯克**: "我们现在有几台Optimus在我们的工厂内运行，执行有用任务——物料搬运、零件检查、简单装配操作。它们能做的事情仍然有限，但改进速度令人印象深刻。我们目标在2026年进行有限的外部生产。规模化成本目标低于2万美元。这可能是史上最大的产品——比汽车业务还大。有用人形机器人的需求基本上是无限的。"
> 风格标记: ["several_units", "impressive_rate", "biggest_product", "unlimited_demand"]

### 片段 3: 储能业务爆发
**Analyst**: "Energy storage deployments were impressive this quarter. Can you talk about the trajectory?"
**分析师**: "本季度储能部署令人印象深刻。能谈谈发展趋势吗？"

**Musk**: "Energy storage is growing faster than any other part of Tesla. Megapack deployments nearly doubled year-over-year. We're sold out through 2025 at this point. The demand for grid-scale storage is just enormous as the world transitions to renewable energy. Tesla Energy is going to be a very significant business — I wouldn't be surprised if it eventually becomes bigger than the automotive business. The world needs terawatt-hours of storage capacity. We're just scratching the surface."
**马斯克**: "储能业务增长速度比Tesla任何其他部分都快。Megapack部署同比几乎翻倍。目前我们已经售罄到2025年。随着世界向可再生能源转型，电网级储能需求巨大。Tesla能源将成为非常重要的业务——我不会惊讶如果它最终变得比汽车业务还大。世界需要太瓦时的储能容量。我们只是触及表面。"
> 风格标记: ["fastest_growing", "sold_out", "bigger_than_auto", "scratching_surface"]

### 片段 4: Robotaxi计划
**Analyst**: "Can you give us an update on the Robotaxi timeline and strategy?"
**分析师**: "能更新一下Robotaxi的时间线和策略吗？"

**Musk**: "We're planning to launch Robotaxi in a limited number of cities in 2025, expanding significantly in 2026. The unit economics are compelling — we estimate the cost per mile at around 20-30 cents, compared to $1-2 for traditional ride-hailing. The fleet is already there — every Tesla with FSD capability can become a robotaxi with a software update. This is going to be a massive new revenue stream. The total addressable market is in the trillions."
**马斯克**: "我们计划在2025年在有限数量的城市推出Robotaxi，2026年大幅扩展。单位经济性很吸引人——我们估计每英里成本约20-30美分，而传统网约车是1-2美元。车队已经存在——每辆有FSD能力的Tesla可以通过软件更新成为robotaxi。这将成为一个巨大的新收入来源。总可寻址市场以万亿计。"
> 风格标记: ["limited_cities", "compelling_economics", "already_there", "trillions"]

### 片段 5: 下一代车辆平台
**Analyst**: "What can you tell us about the next-generation vehicle platform?"
**分析师**: "你能告诉我们关于下一代车辆平台的信息吗？"

**Musk**: "We're developing a next-generation manufacturing platform that will dramatically reduce the cost of vehicle production. The goal is to cut manufacturing costs by 50% compared to our current platforms. This will allow us to produce a vehicle that's more affordable while still being excellent. We're also working on a purpose-built Robotaxi vehicle that's optimized for autonomous ride-hailing — no steering wheel, lower cost, higher durability. I think this could be the product that really accelerates the transition to sustainable transport."
**马斯克**: "我们正在开发下一代制造平台，将大幅降低车辆生产成本。目标是将制造成本比我们当前平台降低50%。这将使我们能够生产一辆更实惠但仍然出色的车辆。我们还在开发一款专为自动驾驶网约车设计的Robotaxi车辆——没有方向盘，成本更低，耐用性更高。我认为这可能是真正加速向可持续交通过渡的产品。"
> 风格标记: ["50_percent_cost", "more_affordable", "purpose_built", "accelerate_transition"]

### 片段 6: 产量与交付展望
**Analyst**: "What's the production outlook for 2025?"
**分析师**: "2025年的生产展望如何？"

**Musk**: "We're targeting modest growth in vehicle deliveries for 2025 as we prepare for the next-generation manufacturing platform. We don't want to ramp old models too much when we have a much more efficient manufacturing process coming. But the key story for Tesla in 2025 isn't vehicle volume — it's FSD, energy storage, and the services business. The software and services margin is much higher than the vehicle margin. As FSD and Robotaxi scale, the financial profile of Tesla changes dramatically."
**马斯克**: "我们目标在2025年车辆交付量适度增长，因为我们正在准备下一代制造平台。我们不想在有一个更高效制造流程即将到来时过多提升旧车型产量。但Tesla 2025年的关键故事不是车辆数量——而是FSD、储能和服务业务。软件和服务的利润率远高于车辆利润率。随着FSD和Robotaxi规模化，Tesla的财务状况将发生巨大变化。"
> 风格标记: ["modest_growth", "next_generation", "key_story", "dramatically_changes"]

### 片段 7: AI训练基础设施
**Analyst**: "How much are you investing in AI training compute?"
**分析师**: "你在AI训练计算上投资了多少？"

**Musk**: "We're investing heavily in AI training infrastructure — tens of thousands of NVIDIA GPUs and our own custom silicon. The amount of compute needed for FSD training is enormous and growing. We're also building out our own AI supercomputer called Dojo, which is designed specifically for Tesla's AI workloads. The investment is significant — multiple billions — but it's necessary. AI is the core technology that enables everything we're doing at Tesla."
**马斯克**: "我们在AI训练基础设施上大量投资——数万块NVIDIA GPU和我们自己的定制芯片。FSD训练所需的计算量巨大且在增长。我们还在构建自己的AI超级计算机Dojo，专为Tesla的AI工作负载设计。投资是显著的——数十亿美元——但这是必要的。AI是使我们在Tesla做的一切成为可能的核心技术。"
> 风格标记: ["heavily_investing", "enormous_compute", "Dojo", "core_technology"]
